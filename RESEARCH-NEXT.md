# Little Mouse research handoff

Reviewed 2026-11-17. Recommendations only; no gameplay implementation changed. Preserve realistic 3D prey, aggressive motion, press reactions including blood, phone companion, OTA and native iPad delivery. Research does not validate or invalidate those product requirements.

## Prioritized integration requirements

1. **P0 — Make aggressive motion tunable.** Separate speed, burst frequency, turning and pause controls; include straight escape runs as a testable pattern. Keep realistic prey while comparing motion settings without changing the model. Acceptance: the owner can reduce pace or pause immediately without restarting. **Evidence:** Ellis & Wells found greater attention to moving animate/inanimate footage than human footage or a blank screen in 125 shelter cats; viewing averaged 6.10% and declined over exposure. **Limit:** passive television, not interactive iPad play; no optimum speed or demonstrated 3D advantage. [Publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0168159107003619), [university record](https://pure.qub.ac.uk/en/publications/the-influence-of-visual-stimulation-on-the-behaviour-of-cats-hous/).

2. **P0 — Give both devices reliable human control.** Phone companion and local iPad should expose steering, pace, pause and end, with visible current state. Acceptance: local controls remain usable without a companion connection. **Evidence:** Payne et al.'s MewTube comparison involved 28 people and 16 cats; human enjoyment was similar across tablet/toy conditions, but toys yielded stronger reported connection. **Limit:** curated video with human speed adjustment, not a test of remote control, 3D rendering or feline enjoyment. Companion controls are our engineering inference. [Author institution's abstract](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/365042/).

3. **P0 — Make contact consequential and sessions finishable.** A press should cause a spatially aligned reaction; distinguish hit, caught and respawn states. Preserve blood effects as requested. End should stop prey spawning and allow a physical-toy handoff. Acceptance: an accepted catch visibly interrupts flight; end cannot silently begin another chase. **Evidence:** Kogan & Grigg's 618-guardian laser survey found associations with reported repetitive behaviors. **Limit:** correlation, uncertain temporal order, no tablet experiment and no dose threshold. Neither blood, digital capture nor physical-toy handoff was demonstrated to prevent those behaviors. Treat reactions as interaction design, not a therapeutic claim. [Full primary paper, especially §4 limitations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8388446/).

4. **P1 — Offer deliberate variety, independent of intensity.** Let the owner select prey appearances and movement presets, save a cat's settings, and replay a familiar setup. Do not infer that fewer touches require automatic speed escalation. Acceptance: change appearance without resetting speed; change motion without replacing the asset. **Evidence:** Hall et al. observed habituation to repeated physical toys and renewed play after a contrasting replacement. **Limit:** physical sensory properties are not screen pixels; no preferred hue or digital rotation interval was established. [Publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0168159102001533).

5. **P1 — Evaluate behavior separately from impressions.** Keep touches/catches descriptive. For voluntary play comparisons, record human-observed looking, approach, leaving and return alongside contacts and separate owner ratings. Compare one variable at a time and vary order across repeated sessions; lack of touches alone cannot identify disengagement. Acceptance: summaries never label a touch count “happiness” or “welfare.” Five minutes may remain a configurable product default, never an optimum. **Basis:** screen attention and human experience were different outcomes in [Ellis & Wells](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0168159107003619) and [Payne et al.](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/365042/). This evaluation protocol is a proposal, not a validated feline enjoyment instrument.

## Delivery context and evidence boundaries

Native iPad, phone pairing and OTA are implementation work for the coordinator. As an engineering recommendation, keep saved cat settings and a build/config identifier across updates so behavioral comparisons remain interpretable. No reviewed study compares native versus web delivery, OTA behavior, blood effects, realistic versus stylized 3D prey, or an optimum color, speed or duration. Avoid attributing such claims to these references.

Reference verification: Hall title/authors/year/DOI and findings checked against the publisher's indexed abstract; its DOI direct-open failed. Ellis title/authors/year/pages checked against Queen's University Belfast and findings against the publisher's indexed abstract; a subsequent direct publisher request returned 403. Payne metadata and abstract checked on the University of Glasgow repository; PDF retrieval was unsuccessful, so no additional full-text claims are used. Kogan full text and limitations checked on PMC. These are four primary studies, not secondary pet-advice summaries; Hall/Ellis details here are abstract-level evidence.
